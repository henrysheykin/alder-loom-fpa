Data quality and reconciliations

Every refresh re-reads the raw source extracts, rebuilds the model and runs these checks. Dashboards are published only when the checks pass.

How a refresh works

1 · New extractsCSV exports from Shopify, Shopify Payments, 3PL, QuickBooks, bank, Gusto, Meta, Google, purchasing and planning land in the project folder.
2 · Build the modelThe pipeline joins sources, applies the accounting rules (dispatch-date revenue, no double counting) and computes all metrics from raw data.
3 · ReconcileLedger, bank, processor, inventory, payroll, 3PL and ad platforms are tied out to the cent against each other and the control totals.
4 · PublishThe dashboard updates at the same link. The AI analyst answers from the refreshed data.
