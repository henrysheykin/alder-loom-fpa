    Data sources

    Every simulated system that feeds the model, with table and record counts.

    Demo: connections are simulated with synthetic extracts modeled on a shop-management system, scheduling, inventory and purchasing, billing, a card processor, payroll, QuickBooks-style accounting, the bank, ad platforms and planning files. No native API fidelity is claimed.

    Business events in the case

    Designed events of the synthetic company

    Data quality and reconciliations

    Every build re-reads the raw extracts, rebuilds the model in integer cents and runs these checks. The dashboard is published only when all pass.

    How a refresh works

    1 · New extractsCSV exports from shop management, scheduling, inventory, purchasing, billing, card processor, payroll, ledger, bank, ads and planning.
    2 · Build the modelMonthly partitions are joined by ID, child rows aggregated before joins, money kept in cents. P&L uses period activity, not closing balances.
    3 · ReconcileGL = trial balance, 13 metrics × 24 months × company and 4 locations match the control totals, FY figures and cutoff balances match the company profile.
    4 · PublishThe dashboard updates at the same link and the AI analyst answers from the refreshed data.
