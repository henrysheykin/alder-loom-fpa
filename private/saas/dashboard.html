Revenue vs billing vs cash

Bookings = signed commitments (incl. the 26 Oct future-start contract). Billings = invoices less credit notes. Revenue = service days earned. Cash = captures at the processor, then payouts reaching the bank 4–7 days later. Annual upfront billing builds deferred revenue.

Data sources

Every system that feeds the model, with record counts, tables and the latest actual record date, recomputed on each build.

Demo: connections are simulated with synthetic extracts that model Stripe Billing and Payments, QuickBooks, Gusto, HubSpot, AWS, Meta and Google Ads, product analytics, the bank and FP&A planning files.

Data quality and reconciliations

Every build re-reads the raw extracts, rebuilds the model in integer cents and runs these checks. Dashboards are published only when all pass.

How a refresh works

1 · New extractsCSV exports from billing, payments, contracts, revenue subledger, product analytics, CRM, cloud, payroll, ledger, bank, ads and planning.
2 · Build the modelMonthly partitions are joined, IDs typed as text and money kept in cents. MRR is rebuilt from subscription status and price history at every month end.
3 · ReconcileLedger, trial balance, bank, revenue schedule and payroll tie out to the cent; 38 metrics × 24 months match the control totals.
4 · PublishThe dashboard updates at the same link and the AI analyst answers from the refreshed data.
