Client profitability

Contribution per client = net fees earned − employer payroll allocated from timesheets − accepted freelancer cost. The allocation re-cuts payroll already in the ledger; it is not an extra expense. Bench time and paid leave stay unallocated, so client contributions add up to gross profit plus that amount.

Data sources

Every system that feeds the model, with record counts, tables and the latest actual record date, recomputed on each build.

Demo: connections are simulated with synthetic extracts that model HubSpot, contracts, timekeeping, Gusto, a freelancer portal, client-owned Meta and Google ad accounts, QuickBooks, the bank and FP&A planning files.

Data quality and reconciliations

Every build re-reads the raw extracts, rebuilds the model in integer cents and runs these checks. Dashboards are published only when all pass.

How a refresh works

1 · New extractsCSV exports from CRM, contracts, timekeeping, payroll, freelancers, ad platforms, delivery approvals, ledger, bank and planning.
2 · Build the modelMonthly partitions are joined, IDs typed as text and money kept in cents. Revenue follows service approval; client media stays outside the agency's books.
3 · ReconcileLedger, subledgers, bank, payroll, freelancers and delivery are tied out to the cent, and 47 metrics × 24 months match the control totals.
4 · PublishThe dashboard updates at the same link and the AI analyst answers from the refreshed data.
