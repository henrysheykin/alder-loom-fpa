    Data sources

    Every simulated system that feeds the model, with table, file and record counts.

    Demo: connections are simulated with synthetic extracts that model a restaurant POS, card processor, DoorDash, Uber Eats, payroll, timekeeping, kitchen inventory, procurement, QuickBooks, the bank, ad platforms and planning files.

    Data quality and reconciliations

    Every build re-reads the raw extracts, rebuilds the model in integer cents and runs these checks. The dashboard is published only when all pass.

    How a refresh works

    1 · New extractsCSV exports from POS, card processor, delivery platforms, payroll, timekeeping, inventory, procurement, ledger, bank, ads and planning.
    2 · Build the modelMonthly partitions are joined, IDs typed as text and money kept in cents. Revenue follows closed checks; marketplace orders are not counted twice.
    3 · ReconcileAn independent verifier checks the package, and 49 metrics × 24 months × 4 entities match the control totals to the cent.
    4 · PublishThe dashboard updates at the same link and the AI analyst answers from the refreshed data.
