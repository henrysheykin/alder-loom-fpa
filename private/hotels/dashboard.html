    Data sources

    Every simulated system that feeds the model, with table, file and record counts.

    Demo: connections are simulated with synthetic extracts that model a hotel PMS, room availability, night audit, channel manager and OTAs, payment gateway, corporate billing, housekeeping operations, payroll, procurement, fixed assets and debt, ad platforms, QuickBooks, the bank and planning files.

    Data quality and reconciliations

    Every build re-reads the raw extracts, rebuilds the model in integer cents and runs these checks. The dashboard is published only when all pass.

    How a refresh works

    1 · New extractsCSV exports from the PMS, night audit, channel manager, gateway, corporate billing, operations, payroll, ledger, bank, ads and planning.
    2 · Build the modelMonthly partitions are joined, IDs typed as text and money kept in cents. Revenue follows consumed nights; bookings, invoices and payouts are never counted as extra sales.
    3 · ReconcileAn independent verifier checks the package, and 47 metrics × 24 months × 4 scopes match the control totals to the cent.
    4 · PublishThe dashboard updates at the same link and the AI analyst answers from the refreshed data.
